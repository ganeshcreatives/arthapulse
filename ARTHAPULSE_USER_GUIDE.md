## 4. The Golden Rule: Understanding Conviction Scores

ArthaPulse rates every stock on a scale of **0 to 100**. To protect investors from low-conviction gambles, we maintain strict thresholding:

### 🟢 Positive Outlook (Score ≥ 90 / 100)
- **What it means**: High-conviction setup where technicals, fundamentals, macro tailwinds, and institutional accumulation are all firing simultaneously.
- **Recommended Action**: **Invest / Accumulate on dips**.
- **Examples**: Leaders like Tata Motors, HAL, Bharat Electronics, or Kotak Bank during strong sector capex cycles.

### 🟡 Wait & Watch (Score 50 – 89 / 100)
- **What it means**: Solid company, but currently consolidating, waiting for earnings results, or facing minor macro headwinds (e.g. rising crude oil).
- **Recommended Action**: **Watch closely**. Do not rush; wait for a confirmed technical breakout or score upgrade to ≥ 90.

### 🔴 Negative Outlook (Score < 50 / 100)
- **What it means**: Bearish technical breakdown, elevated debt/valuation, or severe sector headwinds.
- **Recommended Action**: **Avoid / Trim existing positions**. Preserve your capital.

## 7. Disciplined Risk Management: Targets & Safety Exits

Every successful investor knows that **managing downside risk is more important than chasing gains**. ArthaPulse gives you two critical risk parameters for every stock:

1. **Target Price (3-Month Horizon)**:
   - A mathematically sound upside expectation based on technical resistance and average volatility. It prevents you from getting greedy and holding onto a stock when it has reached fair value.
2. **Safety Exit Price (Stop-Loss)**:
   - Placed just below key 20-day EMA support levels (usually 6% to 7.5% below current market price).
   - **Crucial Rule**: If the stock drops below this price, exit the position. This protects your hard-earned capital from prolonged 20–40% bear market drawdowns.
